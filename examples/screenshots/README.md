# Screenshots of the frontends

`images/` holds screenshots of the two example frontends, `srl-tui`
([`../python-frontend/`](../python-frontend/)) and `srl-web`
([`../web-frontend/`](../web-frontend/)), studying the small demo store
in `store/`. The rest of this folder takes them again: `capture-tui.sh`,
`tui.html` and `shoot.mjs`.

Taking them is written as instructions for an agent, in
[`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md): ask one to read it and take new screenshots. It
also says how they are made and which choices (theme, width, fonts) they
follow now. Tried on Linux only.
