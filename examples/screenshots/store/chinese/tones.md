# Mandarin tones

Mandarin has four tones and a neutral one. The same syllable with a
different tone is a different word:

| Tone | Contour | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | high, level | mā 妈 | mother |
| 2nd | rising | má 麻 | hemp |
| 3rd | dipping | mǎ 马 | horse |
| 4th | falling | mà 骂 | to scold |

**Tone sandhi:** two third tones in a row become second + third:
你好 is written nǐ hǎo but said ní hǎo.
