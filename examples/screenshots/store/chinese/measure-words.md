# Measure words

A number can't stand directly before a noun in Chinese: a **measure word**
(量词) goes between them, chosen by the shape or kind of the thing.

- 个 (gè): the general one, for people and most things.
- 本 (běn): bound volumes, such as books.
- 张 (zhāng): flat things, such as tables, paper, tickets.
- 条 (tiáo): long, thin, flexible things, such as rivers, fish, roads.
