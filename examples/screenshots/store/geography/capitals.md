# Capitals that aren't the largest city

Many countries put their capital somewhere other than their biggest city,
often as a compromise between rivals or to develop the interior.

- **Canberra** was built because Sydney and Melbourne each wanted to be
  the capital of Australia.
- **Brasília** was inaugurated in 1960 to move the government inland,
  away from Rio de Janeiro on the coast.
- **Ottawa** was chosen by Queen Victoria in 1857, between English-speaking
  Toronto and French-speaking Montreal.
