# 〜をものともせず

**Undeterred by, braving (adversity).** Written, slightly formal. The
subject overcomes something that would stop most people, and the speaker
admires it. Not used about oneself.

- 周囲の反対をものともせず、彼女は起業した。
  Despite everyone's opposition, she started a company.
- 嵐をものともせず、漁師たちは海へ出た。
  Braving the storm, the fishermen went out to sea.

Compare **〜にもかかわらず**, which is neutral: it only says that something
happened in spite of something else, without admiration.

Q: What does 〜をものともせず express, and can you use it about yourself?
A: Overcoming adversity, undeterred by it, with the speaker's admiration.
  Not about yourself.
