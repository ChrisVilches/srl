# なるべく

**As much as possible, if possible.** An adverb, common in speech and
writing. It asks for, or promises, a reasonable effort, not the utmost:
"where you can". Often with requests (〜てください) and intentions (〜ようにする).

- なるべく早く返事をください。
  Please reply as soon as you can.
- 夜はなるべく甘いものを食べないようにしている。
  I try not to eat sweets at night, as far as I can.

Compare **できるだけ**, which means the same but sounds a little stronger:
"as much as one is able to", to the limit of one's ability.
