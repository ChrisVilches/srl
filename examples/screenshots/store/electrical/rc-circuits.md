# RC circuits

A capacitor charging through a resistor follows an exponential curve with
**time constant** τ = RC.

- After 1τ the capacitor reaches about **63%** of the supply voltage.
- After 5τ it is considered fully charged (over 99%).

```srl id:pldhp1
Q: What is the time constant of a 10 kΩ resistor with a 100 µF capacitor?
A: τ = RC = 10,000 × 0.0001 = 1 s.
```
